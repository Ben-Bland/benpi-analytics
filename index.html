benpi analytics

A private tool for one person.

benpi analytics is a personal script that collects performance statistics for videos published on its owner's own YouTube channel. It runs on a single computer at home and is used by one person: the owner of that channel.

It reads two things, both belonging to the account that authorizes it:

  • the list of videos published on the channel, with their publication times
  • view counts, watch time, and audience retention data for those videos

It records those numbers so they can be compared over time. It does not post, edit, delete, or change anything. It has no other users and no public interface.
